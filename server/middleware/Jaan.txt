nee pai naakunna prema --
    padhaallo chepdhamante aksharalu mayamauthunnai
    kallatho chepdhamante nee roopanni thana ninda nimpukunna kanupapa baruvekkuthundhi
    poni suryuditho rayabaram pampi chepdhamante na prema velugu mundhu thana velugu chinnadauthundhani siggupadi suryudemo thana bharya kongu chivarana dhakkunnaadu
    sare ani gaalitho kaburu pamapi chepdhamante ne prema thaakidini cherche sakthi naalo ledhani vaayuvu thala dinchukuni gandhavathi ki parugulu theesaadu
Inni godavalendhukani na premanu na manasutho chepthunna ne manasutho ardham chesuko
